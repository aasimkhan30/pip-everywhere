# Privacy Policy for PiP Everywhere

Effective date: July 26, 2026

PiP Everywhere is designed to work entirely on your Windows device. It does not
transmit, sell, or share personal information. Its limited on-device data
access and storage are described below.

## Information accessed on your device

To identify browser picture-in-picture windows, the app temporarily examines
visible top-level window titles, window class names, and process names. It also
checks whether supported browsers are installed or running. This information is
used only in memory to identify and pin picture-in-picture windows. It is not
recorded or transmitted.

PiP Everywhere does not access browser tabs, URLs, browsing history, cookies,
page content, video content, documents, contacts, location, microphone, camera,
or account information.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores only its settings, such as whether it is enabled and which
browsers you selected. If startup fails, it may create a local diagnostic log
containing technical error details and local file paths. These files remain on
your device, are protected by your Windows account permissions, and are never
sent automatically. Settings remain until you change or delete them; diagnostic
logs remain until you delete them or remove the app's data.

You can stop all window inspection by turning off PiP Everywhere or quitting
the app. You can remove its locally stored files by uninstalling the app and
deleting any remaining PiP Everywhere app-data folder.

## Network use and disclosure

The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, online account, or developer
backend. It does not disclose information to the developer or to third parties.
Windows, Microsoft Store, or GitHub may independently process download and
update information under their own privacy policies.

## Children

PiP Everywhere does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone
else.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Questions can
be submitted through the
[PiP Everywhere issue tracker](https://github.com/aasimkhan30/pip-everywhere/issues).
